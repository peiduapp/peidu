Help

How do I read something?

Pick Camera, Photo, or Text at the top. On the camera, tap the shutter to freeze a page, or hold it for Closer Look. On Text, tap Paste, then Read.

How do I hear it?

Tap a word, tap ▶ beside a sentence, or tap Play all. Voice and speed are in Settings → Speech. Pinyin is coloured by tone: mā má mǎ mà ma.

How do I copy a sentence?

Press and hold it, then choose Chinese, pinyin, English, or all three.

What is Closer Look?

A stronger scan for small print, dense pages, and Zhuyin books. On the camera, hold the shutter about one second. On Photo, tap the magnifier beside Choose Photos.

What do grey characters and □ mean?

Both mean "check this against the page". A grey character is one Peidu isn't sure of. A □ marks a spot where it thinks a character is missing, which can happen on pages with Zhuyin. Tap the word to fix it.

It says "Some lines may be missing."

The quick scan couldn't read all of the page, often because of Zhuyin or small print. Tap Closer Look beside Resume (on Photo, the magnifier) to read it again with the stronger scan.

Peidu misread a character.

Tap the word, then Edit (or Check on a grey one). Pick a suggestion, type how it sounds (jian), or delete it.

Can it read a whole book?

Yes, with Reader mode: on Photo, choose up to 20 pages at once, or tap Add page after each camera capture. Swipe to turn the page; Save book keeps them all. A free book uses one Closer Look per page.

What does Premium include?

Unlimited Closer Look, Reader mode, Simplified ⇄ Traditional, and saves with export, for one purchase with no subscription. Reading, pinyin, English, and speech stay free, and you can try 10 Closer Looks, 10 saves, and a 3-page book first.

New phone, no Premium?

Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The only exception is English translation: the first time, iOS downloads its language pack.

No Chinese voice, or it sounds robotic?

Peidu uses the Mandarin voices on your iPhone. Settings → Speech → More voices shows how to download a better one.

Which iPhones?

Any iPhone on iOS 26 or later.

Anything else: hello@peidu.app. A screenshot helps, since Peidu collects nothing.
