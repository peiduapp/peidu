Help

How do I read something?

Point the camera, tap the photo icon for a picture or screenshot, or tap the text icon to paste. Tap the shutter to freeze a page; hold it for Closer Look.

How do I hear it?

Tap a word, tap ▶ beside a sentence, or tap Play all. Voice and speed are in Settings. Pinyin is coloured by tone: mā má mǎ mà ma.

What is Closer Look?

A stronger scan for small print, dense pages, and Zhuyin books. Hold the shutter about one second. Dotted underlines mark characters to check against the page.

Peidu misread a character.

Tap the word, then Edit. Pick a suggestion, type how it sounds (jian), or delete it.

What does Premium include?

Unlimited Closer Look, Simplified ⇄ Traditional, and unlimited saves with export. One-time purchase, no subscription. Reading, pinyin, English, and speech stay free. You get 10 Closer Looks and 10 saves to try first.

New phone, no Premium?

Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The only exception is English translation: the first time, iOS downloads its language pack.

No Chinese voice, or it sounds robotic?

Peidu uses the Mandarin voices on your iPhone. Settings in Peidu shows how to download a better one.

Which iPhones?

Any iPhone on iOS 26 or later.

Anything else: hello@peidu.app. A screenshot helps, since Peidu collects nothing.
