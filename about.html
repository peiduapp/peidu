About Pindu

Pindu (拼读, pīndú, “sound it out and read”) is an iPhone app for people who can already speak Chinese but never learned to read it.

Why it exists

Language apps start from zero. Camera translators skip to English. Pindu puts the sounds you already know on top of the characters you don't.

How it works

Everything runs on your iPhone. No server, no account.

  • Reading: Apple Vision; Closer Look runs PaddleOCR through ONNX Runtime.
  • Pinyin and meanings: CC-CEDICT, built in.
  • English: Apple Translation and Apple Intelligence, on-device.
  • Speech: your iPhone's Mandarin voices.
